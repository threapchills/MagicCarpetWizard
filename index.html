THE CARPET RIDER’S COMPANION

A little know-how.

01 / Chase the ground

W climb, S descend. A D steer. Low flight is faster and builds skyfire. Keep clear of walls and towers.

02 / Make a little magic

Aim with the mouse; hold click to cast. Collect floating spell crystals. Fire, frost, lightning and echo combine automatically.

03 / Find your flow

SPACE barrel roll above 4m. Near misses, golden rings and defeats build a chain. Touch gold wisps to keep it alive.

04 / Burn a little brighter

Hold SHIFT with at least 25 skyfire to soar fast at any height. Tricks and defeats refuel you. Ward crystals restore a heart.
