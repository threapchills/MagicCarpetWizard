THE CARPET RIDER’S COMPANION

A little know-how.

01 / Chase the ground

W climb, S descend. A D steer. Low flight is faster and builds skyfire. Hold SHIFT to boost. Skim the glowing cliff faces at 6–46 m altitude for extra speed and skyfire. Keep clear of buildings.

02 / Make a little magic

Aim and hold click to cast. 1 Fireball, 2 Lightning, 3 Wind blast; Q cycles. Wind clears enemy shots and fans burning enemies. Frost sets up fire shatters.

03 / Find your flow

SPACE barrel roll above 4m. Near misses, golden rings and defeats build a chain. Touch gold wisps to keep it alive.

04 / Burn a little brighter

Kill monsters for loot. Spell levels persist through the run; Rapid Fire, Fury, Focus and Overdrive are timed boosts. Bosses fly independently: attack, skim past or boost away. Scarabs have two fragile wards; other bosses are vulnerable immediately. Ward crystals heal.
